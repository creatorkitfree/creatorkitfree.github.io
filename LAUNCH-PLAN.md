# CreatorKit Free — $0 launch and first-$100 experiment

## What is implemented
- Eight browser-only utilities; four unique searchable tutorial pages.
- No paid API, server, customer support, or payments required to operate tools.
- No ads, analytics or affiliate links are active. Do not claim the site is monetized.

## Launch, day 1
1. Upload **contents** of this folder to a new public GitHub repository (or Cloudflare Pages).
2. Enable GitHub Pages: Settings > Pages > Deploy from branch > main / root.
3. Visit the public URL on mobile and desktop; test each image tool and guide.
4. Replace the temporary privacy/contact wording with a real contact method before applying to ad networks.
5. After URL is live, generate sitemap.xml using that exact base URL, update robots.txt with Sitemap: URL, and submit sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools.

## 2 hours/day for 21 days (no outreach)
- 40 min: build or improve one useful tool or guide based on real user questions.
- 40 min: make one original, genuinely helpful demo video or tutorial; publish on an account you own (no spam, no cold DMs).
- 25 min: review Search Console queries, pages indexed, click-through and errors.
- 15 min: QA mobile usability, accessibility, page speed and fix issues.

## Revenue strategy
- First priority: indexed pages and engaged visitors. Do not expect organic search traffic immediately.
- Apply to legitimate affiliate programs only if relevant and permitted in your country; include clear affiliate disclosures on every affected page. Avoid fake reviews.
- Apply for display ads only when the site has enough original content and satisfies the network’s policies. Do not click your own ads or buy artificial traffic.
- Track earned revenue separately from paid-out cash; payout schedules and thresholds vary.
- $100 in 21 days is an experiment, NOT a reliable forecast. It may produce $0.

## Measurement
Record daily: indexed pages, search impressions, clicks, unique visitors (if consent-compliant analytics enabled), outbound affiliate clicks, approved commissions, paid commissions, ad revenue, time invested. At day 7 and day 21 decide based on evidence.

## Important product limitations
- Thumbnail tool resizes to 1280×720 and can stretch mismatched aspect ratios; crop images externally first for best quality.
- Browser-supported formats vary; large images may exhaust memory.
- Password generator uses browser crypto, but this site is not a password manager.
- Ad/affiliate approvals and eligibility are not guaranteed.
