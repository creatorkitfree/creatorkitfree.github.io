FREE AI STUDIO / PERSON CUTOUT

Remove a person's background in one click.

AI person segmentation runs on your device. Best for photos with one or more people; this version does not reliably isolate products, animals, or other objects.

Choose a photo containing a person. The AI model downloads when you first process an image.

How it works

Choose a photo, tap Remove background, wait for the on-device AI model, and download your PNG. Processing time depends on your phone. The image stays in your browser; a model file is fetched from a third-party CDN.

Powered by MediaPipe Image Segmenter and Google's person segmentation model. No server-side image upload. The segmentation edge may be imperfect, especially with hair and complex backgrounds.
