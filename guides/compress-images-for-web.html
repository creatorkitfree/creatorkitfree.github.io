Compress Images for the Web Without Uploading

Large images can slow down pages. WebP and JPEG with an appropriate quality setting often reduce download sizes.

How to do it

Open the compressor, select an image, start with WebP at quality 70, download and compare appearance and file size. Try 80–90 for detailed photography. Always keep the original.

Does the website upload my files?

No. The tools process your images or text locally in your browser. No account is required.

Things to check

Always review the exported result, preserve your original file, and check the current requirements of the platform where you intend to publish.
