Convert PNG to WebP in Your Browser

WebP can be smaller than PNG for many photographic images, but results vary. Transparent areas can be preserved in WebP.

How to do it

Open Image Converter, choose a PNG, select WebP, choose quality and download. Check text edges and transparency before replacing the original.
