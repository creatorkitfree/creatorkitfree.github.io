YouTube Thumbnail Size: Resize to 1280 × 720

A common recommended YouTube thumbnail size is 1280 × 720 pixels with a 16:9 aspect ratio. Check YouTube’s current upload requirements before publishing.

How to do it

Choose an image you have rights to use. Open the thumbnail tool, upload it, review the 1280 × 720 dimensions and download. If the image has a different aspect ratio, cropping in a photo editor gives better results than stretching.
